Computer Scince Department

The Department of
Computer Science at Air University (AU), operating under the Faculty of Computing and Artificial Intelligence (FCAI), is a premier hub for computing education, research, and technological innovation in Pakistan. Dedicated to producing socially responsible and industry-ready professionals, the department offers a comprehensive portfolio of undergraduate and graduate programs spanning Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Cyber Security, Data Science, and Software Engineering. E quipped with modern computing laboratories and strong industry-academia linkages, it emphasizes hands-on problem-solving, interdisciplinary research, and ethical leadership. Notably, Air University is a pioneer in specialized cybersecurity education—hosting the National Center for Cyber Security—and continues to gain national and international recognition for high-impact research in AI, computer vision, and machine learning.
